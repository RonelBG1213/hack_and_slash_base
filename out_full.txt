out of balance:
  - [knight] a skilled hero finishes only 7/8 runs -- if every stage passes on its own, the heal between stages is too small to sustain a run

Levers, in order. Enemy *durability* first -- an enemy that dies before its
attack cadence lets it swing again applies no pressure however hard it hits.
Then count and placement (tools/make_level.py), then cadence
(PAUSE_AFTER_ATTACK in game/ai.py). For a run that is failing while its
stages pass, the lever is heal_between_stages -- and it belongs to the class,
so it is the right lever for one class failing and the wrong one for all
five. Enemy damage and class hp last: raising damage makes one mistake
lethal, which is a harsher game rather than a tighter one.
